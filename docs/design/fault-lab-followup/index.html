FAULT LAB GUIDE

One step at a time.

  1. 1

    Choose what changes

    Select a component, conductor or named terminal on the circuit.

  2. 2

    Review and apply a fault

    Check the highlighted location. Applied cards keep a visible badge.

  3. 3

    Run the diagnostic

    An open path can produce zero current. Show the accepted outcome or explain the blocking check.

  4. 4

    Read the response

    Inspect current, potential and named events. Unsupported effects remain unavailable.

  5. 5

    Clear, reset or replace

    Remove the injected cause. Reset protection to OFF, turn ON deliberately, or replace damaged items separately.

  6. 6

    Run again to verify

    Compare fresh results. A cleared marker alone does not prove successful repair.
