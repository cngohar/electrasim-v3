## Evidence labels

- **Historical:** dates, cited notebook observations, documented materials and object context.
- **Reconstructed:** the procedural laboratory, camera movement and modular geometry used to make the evidence inspectable.
- **Simulated:** voltage, current, power, brightness, heating interpolation and failure threshold. These values are educational approximations, not notebook measurements.

## Locked accuracy decisions

The hero is an Edison-style 1879 demonstration lamp. It intentionally has no modern Edison screw base. The 22 October values are shown as approximately 113 ohms starting resistance and approximately 140 ohms later resistance, without presenting them as a standardized modern lamp rating.
